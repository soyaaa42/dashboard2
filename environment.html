🚨 Barangay DRRM Readiness

Source: 2024 Tarlac FINAL CBMS — DRRM worksheet. Three independent barangay-level readiness indicators: presence of a disaster/emergency hotline, a written DRRM plan, and a disaster/emergency response team. Each is measured against the same barangay, so their counts are not mutually exclusive and are not summed together.
