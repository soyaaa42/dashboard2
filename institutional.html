📊 Institutional Sector Data Interpretation

The indicators describe reported institutional measures and service coverage. They do not by themselves confirm that plans, teams, hotlines, or services are updated, fully operational, adequately equipped, or regularly tested.

🛡️

Reported DRRM Coverage

Based on the 2024 CBMS DRRM dataset, 508 of 511 barangays (99.41%) separately reported having a written DRRM plan, and 508 barangays (99.41%) separately reported having a disaster or emergency response team. Emergency-hotline availability is lower at 335 barangays (65.56%), making emergency communication the main reported coverage gap.

📶

Communication Coverage

All assessed barangays report cellphone network availability, which may support emergency communication and digital public services when systems are accessible and operational.

♻️

Service Gaps

Thirty-three barangays report no household garbage collection, concentrated mainly in Camiling and Pura.

🧭 Institutional Planning Recommendations

Recommended PPDO and LGU actions based on the 2024 CBMS institutional indicators.

Emergency Hotline Coverage

Prioritize hotline establishment, testing, and public information campaigns in LGUs with low barangay coverage, especially Concepcion and Gerona. Province-wide, only 335 of 511 barangays (65.56%) reported hotline availability.

Waste-Service Expansion

Prepare phased collection routes, shared equipment arrangements, and monitoring targets for uncovered barangays in Camiling and Pura.

Public Transport Access

Coordinate routes and service schedules for municipalities with the largest shares of households without public transportation access.

Safety Monitoring

Use barangay-level safety perception results to focus lighting, patrol coordination, and community reporting mechanisms.

Digital Public Services

Build on reported universal network availability by assessing signal reliability and expanding online permits, incident reporting, announcements, and feedback channels.

Annual DRRM Validation

Maintain a verified registry of plans, teams, and hotlines, including responsible offices, equipment, contact details, last update dates, and records of drills or operational tests.
